About Our Advocacy

Breaking the stigma and ensuring menstrual equity for all.

Who We Are

We are a passionate group of individuals committed to raising awareness and improving access to menstrual hygiene for girls and women across Ghana. Our organization believes that no one should miss out on education, opportunity, or dignity because of menstruation.

Our Mission

  • Provide free and affordable menstrual hygiene products to underserved communities.
  • Host educational workshops to combat stigma and misinformation.
  • Advocate for policy change to support menstrual health in schools and public systems.

Our Vision

A world where menstruation is never a barrier to education, health, or dignity. We envision a society that embraces inclusivity, empathy, and empowerment for every menstruating person.
